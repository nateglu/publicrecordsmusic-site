Melodic underground rap — Minneapolis, MN

MASTO is a melodic underground rap artist out of Minneapolis. Self-produced and self-directed — he writes, records, mixes, and films everything himself, building a late-night, romantic, lyric-forward sound.

His single “CLOSER” crossed 220,000 streams with no machine behind it, and the new EP FOREVER is pulling a fast-growing creator community into the catalog.
