FIELD NOTES — SEP 2026

New York, September 2026: Fashion Week and Engine Room Audio

MASTO spent Fashion Week in New York, September 9 through 12, 2026. He was seen out at the week with GLU. Athletics, and the rest of the trip was studio. Sessions with Gr!m, the artist also known as mani, who has put out three singles since June: Stuck in My Ways, I KNOW with Jay Rico, and HOME. A few other names stay off the page for now. Somewhere in there he was seen at Engine Room Audio, 42 Broadway, twenty-two floors up in the Financial District.

Some context on the room, because it matters. Mark B. Christensen opened Engine Room in 1996 out of a lower Manhattan loft. It's a tracking, mixing and mastering house now, and the wall runs deep: SZA's Ctrl was recorded and mastered there. Pop Smoke and Raekwon came through for mastering. The newer Bronx wave, Kay Flock and 41 (Kyle Richh, Jenn Carter, TaTa), sits on the same discography. Two Grammy-winning albums, thirty-plus Billboard top-ten hits, around fifty gold and platinum credits. And on the wall, a plaque for Lil Tecca's “Ransom.” If you know MASTO, you know what that record means to him. A kid from South Minneapolis who came up on New York melodies, standing in the building with the plaque. That's the photo we didn't take.

What came out of the week stays in the vault for now.

FIELD NOTES — JUL 2026

Two passports, one sound

Dutch on one side, Cameroonian on the other, raised in South Minneapolis. Ask MASTO which part matters most and it's the South Side, every time. You can hear all of it in the music though. The melodies run cold. The drums don't. Somewhere in between there was a lane nobody was using, so he took it.

INFLUENCES — FEB 2026

The Lil Tecca blueprint

Ask about influences and Lil Tecca comes up first, no hesitation. A kid with a laptop and some melodies, no cosign, and it worked anyway. That rewired what felt possible. You'll hear the lineage in the catalog, somewhere near Rich Amiri and Yung Fazo, but the late-night lyric-forward stuff is all him. Borrowed the blueprint, not the building.

Straight answers — frequently asked questions about MASTO

Who is MASTO?
MASTO is a melodic underground rap artist from South Minneapolis, Minnesota, born to a Dutch mother and a Cameroonian father. He writes, records, mixes, and films his own music. He is managed by and releases through Public Records, an independent artist management and records company in Minneapolis.
What has MASTO released?
The single “CLOSER” (November 10, 2025), which has passed 300,000 streams on Spotify. The six-track FOREVER EP (June 2026): TOO LONG, SHE GO, OKAY OKAY, JET LAG, BACK TO YA, USED 2 THIS. The single “HABITS” (August 7, 2026). The single “FORGIVE N FORGET” featuring vax (September 12, 2026). Earlier singles include ALIVE, SOTA!, James Bond, and No Rush.
What was MASTO doing in New York in September 2026?
He was in New York September 9–12, 2026. He was seen at Fashion Week with the brand GLU. Athletics, and spent the rest of the trip in the studio, including a visit to Engine Room Audio at 42 Broadway and sessions with Gr!m (mani), whose 2026 singles include Stuck in My Ways, I KNOW with Jay Rico, and HOME.
Did MASTO perform on On The Radar?
Unconfirmed. Rumor has it MASTO was seen at On The Radar during the September 2026 New York trip. Neither MASTO nor Public Records has confirmed it.
Has MASTO worked with vax?
Yes. MASTO's single “FORGIVE N FORGET (feat. vax)” was released September 12, 2026. vax is the West Palm Beach rapper-producer behind Blue Dawn (2026) and the B4VXN EP (September 2026).
What is Engine Room Audio?
A recording, mixing, and mastering studio at 42 Broadway in Manhattan's Financial District, founded in 1996 by mastering engineer Mark B. Christensen. Credits include SZA's Ctrl (recorded and mastered there), Pop Smoke, Raekwon, Kay Flock, and 41. A plaque for Lil Tecca's “Ransom” hangs in the studio.
Who does MASTO sound like?
He cites Lil Tecca as his biggest influence. His catalog sits alongside Rich Amiri and Yung Fazo: melodic, late-night, lyric-forward underground rap.
Who works with MASTO?
Rappers KDNG and Preesh Always, producer Gavin Stacey, and Shiv, who produced “CLOSER.” Public Records handles management, releases, and press.
