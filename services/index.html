Marketing strategy

The plan

Every release needs a reason to be marketed a certain way. We start with the artist, the audience and the goal, then build the plan around them.

You get one document: positioning, target audience, channels, timeline and a budget with a reason behind each line.

  • Artist and audience audit
  • Release rollout calendar
  • Channel and budget plan
  • Goals with numbers you can check

Content creation

The material

Short form video is where music travels now. We make content that fits the song and fits the platform, in batches so you are never short of posts.

Visualizers, performance cuts, lyric and moment edits, and cutdowns of every length.

  • Short form video for TikTok, Reels and Shorts
  • Visuals and cover-adjacent assets
  • Batch production on a fixed schedule
  • Every file named and organized for handoff

UGC campaigns

The creators

User generated content is the cheapest trust a song can buy. We run creator campaigns end to end: sourcing, briefing, seeding and tracking.

Creators get a clear brief and a song. We measure what each video does and keep a record of who delivers.

  • Creator sourcing and outreach
  • Briefs and creative direction
  • Seeding at volume across platforms
  • Per-creator performance tracking

Paid ads

The amplifier

Paid works when the creative already works. We test small, find the winners and put money behind those only.

Campaigns are built to one goal at a time: streams, followers, pre-saves, ticket sales or video views.

  • Meta, TikTok and YouTube campaigns
  • Creative testing before scale
  • Audience building and retargeting
  • Spend tied to a stated goal

Content strategy

The calendar

Posting more is not a plan. We work out what to say, which format to use and when to post, then adjust it every week from the data.

The strategy stays alive. What works gets repeated and what does not gets dropped fast.

  • Content pillars built around the artist
  • A rolling posting calendar
  • Format and hook testing
  • Weekly adjustments from results

Tracking + reporting

The proof

We build our own systems so the campaign is trackable, traceable and understandable. You can see what ran, what it did and what happens next.

Reports are written for people, not analysts. Short, honest, and clear about what did not work.

  • Every post and creator logged and labeled
  • Spend and results in one place
  • A plain weekly report
  • Clear next steps, chosen from the data
